About Quicknotes

Quicknotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you capture fast thoughts and daily tasks without distraction. It provides a simple workspace where your ideas are instantly saved and easily managed.

How to use Quicknotes

  1. type your thought into the input box on the home page
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.
  3. Review your saved notes anytime to stay organized

Features

  • Minimalist, distraction-free interface
  • Instant note creation with single-click submission.
  • Real-time note counting to keep track of tasks.
